Pictionary

This one needs someone to play against.

Pick one to draw. Harder words are worth more to everybody.

How scoring works
  • Guessing — 2 points, plus 1 for every 15 seconds still on the clock. Getting there first is worth more than getting there.
  • Drawing — 1 point for every person who got it. A drawing nobody can read is worth nothing.
  • Difficulty — the word you pick adds a bonus on top, for the guessers and the artist: nothing for Easy, +2 for Trickier, +4 for Hard. Harder words also get longer on the clock — 60, 75 and 90 seconds — because a scene takes setting up.
  • With 20 seconds left the first letter is revealed, so a drawing nobody can read isn't just a wait for the clock.
