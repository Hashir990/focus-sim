Delete your account

How to remove your focUS simulator account and everything stored for it.

In the app

Open the menu, then Your focus, and use Delete account in the account panel. It asks for your password, tells you what is about to go, and then removes the account and everything the server holds for it. The device is left signed out, as it was before you ever signed in.

By email

If you cannot reach the app — a lost device, an uninstalled copy — email hashirniamatullah@gmail.com from the address the account was made with and ask for it to be deleted. It is read by a person, and nothing else is needed from you.

What goes

The account itself, the email address and username on it, and the progress stored against it: your focus history, your embers and what you have bought with them, your saved games, and your friends list. Deleting is not deactivating — there is nothing left to sign back into.

Anything held only on your device is not on the server to delete; to clear that too, use Reset progress in the app's menu on each device.

What is kept

Nothing. Data on the server is kept while the account exists and no longer. The full picture is in the privacy policy.
