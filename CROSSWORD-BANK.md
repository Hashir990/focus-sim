## What the generator can actually reach for

The tables further down list the **hand-written clue table**. That is a subset. A clue is only written by hand once an answer lands in a shipped puzzle, and long entries rarely do — bars cut most lines into pieces of three to six — so the sevens, eights and nines look bare below while the pool holds thousands of them.

Anything in the pool without a hand-written clue ships with a clue trimmed from its WordNet definition, which is why `rebuild-bank.py --clues` exists: it lists exactly those, and they are what to rewrite next.

---

`(x3)` means the answer is in three shipped puzzles. `(unused)` means it is clued and ready but has not landed in a grid yet. Answers with two clues show both, one per line; `crossClue` picks between them by puzzle index so a given puzzle always shows the same one.
